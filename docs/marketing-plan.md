# The Biological Edge: marketing plan

## 1. Positioning

**Who it's for:** men 40 to 60 who feel worn down (stressed, tired, out of shape) and are willing to do the work if someone says plainly what the work is.

**The promise:** get your edge back. Energy, strength and a calm mind, rebuilt after 40.

**Why us:** Todd is the audience. He is 51, spent years anxious, exhausted and unhealthy, and fixed it in a specific order. Most content in this space is either written for 25-year-olds or selling a pill. We are neither.

**The method (use these five words everywhere):**

1. **Mind:** stress and anxiety first
2. **Strength:** three sessions a week
3. **Recovery:** sleep, heat, rest
4. **Fuel:** protein first, calmer gut
5. **Measure:** bloodwork and a few numbers

**Voice:** first person, plain, a bit dry. Says "the research is thin" when it is. Never promises a result.

## 3. Channels

**Home base: YouTube long-form.** One video a week, 8 to 15 minutes, Todd on camera. Searchable for years, builds the most trust, and converts best for affiliate links.

**Distribution: one vertical short, four places.** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels all take the same file. Post natively to each (no watermarks).

**Owned: the weekly email.** One email every week: one idea, one link to the new video, one product mention at most.

**Later:** X and LinkedIn need text-first content written for them. Revisit after 90 days.

## 5. The viral re-engineering workflow

For faceless shorts. Run it once a week and produce the week's batch in one sitting.

1. **Find.** Pull the top-performing recent shorts in the niche: men's health over 40, strength, sleep, sauna, supplements, bloodwork.
2. **Break down.** For each one, note the hook (first two seconds), the structure, the pacing, the visual format, and the call to action.
3. **Choose the pattern, not the video.** Pick three or four formats that are working, for example "three mistakes", "I did X for 30 days", "what the study actually found", "stop doing X, do Y".
4. **Write original.** New script on that pattern about one of our five levers, ending with one product or the free reset.
5. **Produce.** Original visuals (generated footage and B-roll) and Todd's cloned voice.
6. **Post and log.** Record views, watch time and clicks per video. Double down on formats that work, drop the ones that don't.

**The line we don't cross:** we borrow structure, never footage, scripts, music or another creator's likeness. Re-uploads and close copies get accounts struck.

## 7. Affiliate rollout

1. **Now:** the shop is live with honest product pages. Buttons point to an Amazon.ca search until we have tracked links.
2. **Once the site is live with content:** apply to Amazon Associates (Canada first, then US). It is usually the easiest approval and covers equipment and most supplements.
3. **Next:** apply directly to the brands Todd actually uses, plus wearables and at-home lab testing. Direct programmes usually pay more than Amazon.
4. **For each approval:** paste the link into `src/data/products.ts`. The button and the affiliate label update automatically.

**Rule:** choose the product first, look for a programme second. Swap a generic listing for a named brand only when Todd uses it or has tested it.

## 10. Rules we keep

- **Disclose every affiliate link,** on the page and in video descriptions ("some links are affiliate links").
- **No disease claims.** Supplements "support" things. They do not treat, cure or prevent.
- **TRT and peptides:** Todd can describe his own experience under a clinic's care. We never sell them, tell people to take them, or name a drug brand. Have this content checked against Health Canada's advertising rules before the first video on it goes out.
- **No invented testimonials, before-and-afters or numbers.** Real ones only, with permission.
- **Label AI-generated video** where the platform asks for it.

## 11. What to measure

| Number | Where | 90-day target to aim at |
|---|---|---|
| Email subscribers | Email provider | The main number. Set the target after the first two weeks of data. |
| Sign-up rate on `/start/` | Site analytics | Improve month over month |

| Average view duration | YouTube Studio | Rising across the first 12 videos |
| Shorts with above-median views | Each platform | Find 2 to 3 repeatable formats |
| Waitlist sign-ups per product | Email provider | Decides the build order |

No targets are set for views or revenue yet. There is no baseline, and made-up targets are worse than none.
